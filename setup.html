Free Groq API Key Setup

⚡ 180ms ULTRA-FAST STREAMING

MergeIt AI uses Groq LPU (Language Processing Unit) hardware. Groq processes text at over 500 tokens per second — generating complete, structured answers before your interviewer even finishes pausing.

100% Free Developer Tier (No Credit Card)

Groq provides a generous free tier with zero payment details required. Using your personal API key gives you dedicated rate limits, zero throttling, and maximum privacy.

Create Free Key on Groq Console
Opens: https://console.groq.com/keys
Step-by-Step Instructions Takes under 60 seconds
1
Sign In to Groq Cloud Visit console.groq.com/keys and log in with your Google or GitHub account.
2
Generate API Key Click the green "Create API Key" button. Enter any name (e.g. MergeIt) and click Submit.
3
Copy Your Secret Key Copy the generated key beginning with gsk_... to your clipboard. (Note: Groq displays it only once).
4
Paste into MergeIt AI Settings In the MergeIt AI HUD, click the Gear Icon (⚙️) in the top header. Paste your key into the Groq API Key field and click Save Changes.
That's it! MergeIt AI will immediately stream answers using your private Groq LPU engine.

Audio Loopback Configuration

Transcribing Interviewer Audio in Real Time

MergeIt AI captures audio using Windows audio loopback so it transcribes the voices of other meeting participants through your headphones or speakers.

One-Touch Toggle (Ctrl + L)

Press Ctrl + L or click the Audio / Loopback button in the HUD to start or pause listening.

Headphones & Meeting Audio

Ensure your meeting platform (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams) audio output device is set to your active headphones so MergeIt captures the interviewer's speech with zero echo.

Click-to-Solve

As questions are spoken, candidate chips appear in the left feed. Click any transcript chip or press Ctrl + Enter to stream the answer instantly.

Tip: Test by opening any YouTube technical interview and pressing Ctrl + L.
