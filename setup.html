Create & Connect Your Free Groq API Key

⚡ 180ms ULTRA-FAST

MergeIt AI is powered by Groq LPU Inference. Groq delivers answers at over 500 tokens per second — meaning when your interviewer finishes asking a question, your answer appears on your teleprompter in less than 300 milliseconds.

Why Create Your Own API Key?

Groq provides a 100% Free Developer Tier with zero credit card required. Using your personal free key gives you unlimited private rate limits with zero throttling during high-stakes interviews.

1
Go to the Groq Cloud Console: Visit https://console.groq.com/keys and log in with your Google or GitHub account.
2
Generate a New API Key: Click the green "Create API Key" button. Give it a name (e.g. MergeIt-Copilot) and click Submit.
3
Copy Your Key: Copy the key that begins with gsk_.... Keep it safe.
4
Paste into MergeIt AI Settings: In the MergeIt AI floating HUD, click the Settings Cog (⚙️) in the top header. Select Provider: Groq, paste your key into Groq API Key, and click "Save Settings".

04

Audio Listener & Real-Time Interview Transcription

MergeIt AI listens to the interviewer's voice and transcribes questions in real time using your system audio loopback.

Toggle Listening: Press Ctrl + L or click the sound wave icon in the top header. When active, the equalizer wave bars animate in green.
Headphones Configuration: If you are wearing headphones during a Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet call, ensure your Windows sound settings have the meeting output set to your active headphones so the system audio loopback captures the interviewer's speech.
Click-to-Solve: As the interviewer speaks, transcript chips appear in the left feed. Click any transcript chip or press Ctrl + Enter to stream the optimal answer instantly.
